# Visual reference: what LINE 13 borrows

Method: `ffmpeg` sampled 8 evenly spaced frames from each 180 s reference (contact sheets viewed; frames not committed, not redistributed). Sources: `WhatsApp Video 2026-11-03 at 10.49.16.mp4` (848x480, a heritage/inscription walkthrough) and `Skill DNA video.mp4` (1924x1080, product walkthrough with burned-in captions). Only ideas are borrowed. No footage, logos, text or layouts are copied; every LINE 13 asset is original CSS/SVG/Web Audio.

## What the frames show

Video A (heritage): near-black warm-brown ground (not pure black); one serif display face for big numbers and section titles ("1,952", "9 in 10") over a small sans label in letter-spaced caps; a single accent colour per scene (amber, then green only for "matches"); a dot-matrix that fills in to prove a number; slow photographic background dimmed behind dark glass panels; a small kicker label above each title; photo credit set tiny in a corner.

Video B (Skill DNA): dark panel UI with a single saturated highlight (yellow active tab); captions as a low, centred, serif line with the current phrase highlighted; ordered stages shown as numbered nodes joined by a line; a cursor/pointer that lands on the thing being discussed; content dims around the focus.

## What to borrow, and how it lands in LINE 13

| Idea | Observed | LINE 13 application |
|---|---|---|
| Pacing | One idea per beat, holds long enough to read, no stacked effects | Each haunting event gets one dominant cue; entrance stays under 7 s; the sigil reveal is one 4 s beat, not a montage |
| Typography | Serif display + tiny letter-spaced caps kicker + calm sans body | Brass-plate caps for state (existing), serif ticket and ending headlines, kicker line above each ending title |
| Lighting | Warm dark ground, dimmed glass panels, one coloured light at a time | Walnut ground, vignette that deepens with possession level, lamp glow is the only saturated light; green phosphor reserved for Line 13 |
| Proof by fill | Dots fill to show a quantity | Routed cords resolve into the sigil one chord at a time, nodes lighting as each route is drawn |
| Caption style | Low centred line, active phrase highlighted | Existing rail glass subtitle stays low and centred; the line being spoken is the highlighted one; possessed lines render in the Line 13 colour |
| Motion | Slow dim/zoom, nothing loops without meaning | Slow camera drift only during the entrance; interference only on crossed lines and Line 13; everything stops under reduced motion |
| Transitions | Cross-dissolve to a new scene with a focus pull | Board dims and the sigil fades in over it; endings arrive as a dissolve, not a hard cut |
| Focus | Surroundings dim around the subject | The active caller's jack and ticket stay lit; other lines recede while a cord is in hand |
| Credit/legibility | Tiny credit line, never over content | Settings strip stays small and unobtrusive; 320 px layout drops secondary text before it drops state words |

## Rules kept from the steering docs

Motion must carry state; reduced-motion and no-audio paths exist for every effect; no colour-only state (every lamp has a word); the reference videos inform framing only.
