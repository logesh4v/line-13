# LINE 13 Product Steering

LINE 13 is a short, replayable supernatural browser game. It is not a productivity tool, educational dashboard, phone service, analytics product, or generic AI story generator.

## Product promise

The browser becomes an abandoned telephone exchange. The player is the night operator. Ghost callers provide clues, cables create a routing graph, wrong connections let voices cross, and the final circuit reveals why Line 13 knows the operator.

## Non-negotiable experience rules

- Show the switchboard immediately; never lead with a marketing page.
- Teach through the first call; never block play behind documentation.
- Make every control look and behave like part of the exchange.
- Every effect must communicate game state, story escalation, or consequence.
- Keep the first successful playthrough between three and five minutes.
- Keep the complete judge path available without login, backend, model call, camera, or microphone.
- Prefer one unforgettable transformation over many shallow features.
- Use original project artwork and audio; do not copy campaign or winner assets.

## Tone

Atmospheric, uncanny, restrained, and intelligible. Build anticipation rather than relying on constant jumpscares, gore, or joke copy. Dialogue should sound like a person caught between memory and signal loss.

## Judge thesis

- Resurrection: an obsolete manual exchange works again.
- Potential Impact: a compact, accessible Halloween experience people can finish, replay, and share.
- Technology: deterministic graph solving, event-driven audiovisual state, AWS speech, and a validated agent content pipeline.
- Design: a coherent diegetic interface with no dashboard shell.
- Innovation: physical routing becomes story structure and ritual geometry.
- Presentation: a complete cinematic arc fits under three minutes.
