# LINE 13 Visual Language

## Selected direction

Occult Exchange: black walnut, aged brass, cloth-covered cords, ivory paper, amber lamps, restrained green phosphor, smoke, and deep shadow.

## Hierarchy

1. Ringing lamp and physical jack.
2. Caller identity and clue ticket.
3. Existing cables and emerging ritual geometry.
4. Escalation state and Line 13.
5. Secondary settings such as subtitles, mute, contrast, and reduced motion.

## Forbidden patterns

- Generic hero plus feature cards.
- SaaS navigation, sidebars, statistic tiles, or admin tables.
- Purple-neon Kiro clone styling.
- Emoji used as production icons.
- Halloween stickers added without interaction meaning.
- Native browser tooltips for important explanations.
- Motion that continues without carrying information.
- Colour-only, sound-only, or motion-only state communication.

## Motion

Motion must represent current, ringing, routing, interference, possession, reveal, or physical camera movement. The intro stops within seven seconds. Every longer or repeating effect needs a reduced-motion alternative; decorative motion stops when its scene ends.

## Audio

The soundscape is diegetic: switchboard bells, jack contact, cloth cord movement, relay clicks, line hum, static, room tone, and processed voices. Spoken content always has exact subtitles. Silence is used deliberately before major calls.

## House of Kiro reference handling

Use the supplied videos only for their immersive framing: the visitor enters a themed world, becomes a participant, and experiences Kiro concepts through action. Do not recreate their set, footage, branded props, logos, sequence, or music.
