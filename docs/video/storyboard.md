# LINE 13 Video Plan

## Judge video

**Format:** 16:9, 1080p, public YouTube, target 2:40, hard maximum 2:55.

### Storyboard

- **0:00–0:07 — Cold open:** black screen, exchange bell, brass closure plate, operator-room reveal, Line 01 rings.
- **0:07–0:18 — One sentence:** “A dead telephone exchange has reopened. You have one shift to route its callers before Line 13 calls you.”
- **0:18–0:42 — Teach through action:** answer the first caller, inspect the paper clue, drag a cord, complete a correct route.
- **0:42–1:10 — Escalation:** several lines ring; subtitles and spatial voices overlap; a wrong route causes crossed speech and environmental corruption.
- **1:10–1:36 — Innovation:** show the same connection as a physical cable, graph edge, story change, and haunting cue; briefly show deterministic replay and solver proof.
- **1:36–1:58 — Finale:** completed routes form the ritual; Line 13 rings from the operator's desk; make the final choice and show one ending.
- **1:58–2:15 — Reliability and access:** demonstrate keyboard control, subtitles, mute, reduced motion, mobile layout, and backend-off bundled play.
- **2:15–2:34 — Kiro workflow:** show the exact spec, one custom-agent handoff, a property-test counterexample, the corrected graph, and browser verification.
- **2:34–2:45 — Crew app:** Night Operator agents create, challenge, solve, and package a new episode.
- **2:45–2:50 — End card:** title, category, public URL, GitHub repository, and “Built with Kiro.”

## Vertical teaser

**Format:** 9:16, 25–30 seconds, original footage and sound only.

- Darkness and one ring.
- Door opens into the exchange.
- Three fast plug-and-route cuts synchronized to relay clicks.
- Wrong connection causes a one-second possession transformation.
- Line 13 lights.
- Final card: “Would you answer?” followed by the required Social Blitz framing outside the video caption.

## Creative rules

- Use the supplied House of Kiro Shorts as a pacing and immersion reference, not as source media.
- Show the player entering a world, not scrolling a product page.
- Capture the real application and real interactions.
- Do not fabricate model, graph, agent, test, or deployment behavior.
- Use original or clearly licensed sound and visual assets.
- Keep captions burned in and readable on a phone.
- Record from a clean public build with no credentials, personal data, notifications, or private browser content.
